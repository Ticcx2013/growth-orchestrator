How it works

One vertical slice, built for judgment rather than volume: the model interprets, the rules decide, the outbox acts, the audit log remembers.

  ING --> ST[(State: accounts · contacts · suppressions

  ST --> EL[Eligibility rules
customer · opportunity · suppression · cooldown · cap · ICP]
  EL --> CO[Compliance rules
opt-out ES/PT/EN · injection heuristics]
  CO --> AI[Reply interpreter · Claude
structured output, evidence must be verbatim]
  AI --> VAL{Validation
schema · grounding · dates}
  VAL -. fail .-> REP[one repair round] -.-> VAL
  VAL --> POL[Policy engine
intent × confidence × state → action · auto / assisted / off]
  CO -- opt-out, rule wins --> POL
  POL -- irreversible action --> FR[Freshness check: re-read the CRM] --> OB
  POL --> OB[(Outbox: one idempotency key per action)]
  POL -- ambiguous · low confidence · flagged --> HQ[(Human review queue)]
  OB --> EXE[Dispatcher
retry · backoff · reconcile uncertain outcomes] --> CRM[(CRM / outreach
mock adapter, HubSpot stub)]
  EXE -. dead letter .-> HQ
  ING & EL & CO & AI & POL & EXE --> AUD[(Audit log: stage · reason · prompt and policy versions)]

What the AI may and may not decide

QuestionWho answers
What does this reply mean? Which facts does it state?Model (interprets)
May we contact this account at all?Rules (state + policy)
Did they ask us to stop?Rules first; model is a second opinion
Which action follows from the intent?Policy table
May that action run without a human?Policy mode + confidence gate
Is this a duplicate? Is this event stale?Rules (keys and versions)
Is the CRM still in the state we think?Freshness check (a read, not a guess)
Anything ambiguous, contradictory, or below thresholdHuman
